隐私政策

最后更新：2026年3月15日

五子棋（Gomoku）应用尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明我们如何处理您的信息。

我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息。具体来说：

  • 不需要账号注册或登录
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等个人身份信息
  • 仅在您主动使用「扫描邀请码」功能时访问摄像头，不访问麦克风或通讯录
  • 不追踪您的位置

本地存储数据

应用仅在您的设备本地存储以下数据，用于游戏功能：

  • 玩家昵称（由您输入）
  • 对局记录和胜负统计
  • 游戏设置（音效、振动、难度等）
  • 进行中的对局存档

以上数据仅保存在您的设备上，不会上传至任何服务器。

第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方广告、分析或追踪服务。

网络权限

本应用包含联机对战功能，该功能需要网络连接。网络仅用于玩家之间的游戏数据传输，不用于收集个人信息。

儿童隐私

本应用适合所有年龄段使用。我们不会故意收集13岁以下儿童的任何个人信息。

隐私政策变更

如果我们更新隐私政策，会在此页面发布新版本，并更新页面顶部的日期。

联系我们

如有关于隐私政策的问题，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们：
https://github.com/Ian-Miller/wuziqi/issues

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Gomoku (五子棋) respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

This app does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login required
  • No collection of name, email, phone number, or other personal identifiers
  • Camera is accessed only when you actively use the "Scan Invite QR Code" feature; no microphone or contacts access
  • No location tracking

Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device solely for game functionality:

  • Player nickname (entered by you)
  • Game records and win/loss statistics
  • Game settings (sound, vibration, difficulty, etc.)
  • In-progress game save

All data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Network Permission

The app includes an online multiplayer feature that requires a network connection. The network is used solely for transmitting game data between players and is not used to collect personal information.

Camera Permission

Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a QR code to join an online game. No images or video are stored or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

Contact Us
